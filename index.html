Icestasy · FlagshipIdea to launch

Building the operating system for an ice cream business

Three integrated platforms spanning sales, production and HR, relied upon daily by sales, kitchen, factory and office teams.

70–100orders processed daily on Order Desk
~85%faster order entry (10–15 min to 1–2 min)
Lowerstock-outs and wastage
Read the case study
Problem
Orders from cafés, hotels, retailers and distributors across six regions arrived as unstructured WhatsApp messages. Sales representatives re-keyed each order manually, taking 10–15 minutes apiece, while production lacked real-time visibility of demand, leaving inventory planning to estimation.
Users
15–20 salespeople and six regional heads; kitchen and factory teams; 18 employees on the HRMS, scaling to 40+.
My role
Identified the opportunity, secured the owner's buy-in, defined the product specifications, built the platforms using AI-assisted development, and led rollout and user training.
Decisions
  • Meet users where they are. Customers continue to order via WhatsApp; sales representatives paste the message and the AI parser constructs the order.
  • Accuracy over automation. Ambiguous items require confirmation by the sales representative, and a rule-based fallback maintains continuity if the AI service is unavailable.
  • One source of truth. Sales and production share a single database, so weekly ingredient requirements are calculated from six weeks of actual orders, with alerts when cover falls below 2.5 weeks.
  • Full audit trail. Every stock movement is logged as a transaction rather than overwriting totals, so every figure can be traced.
What shipped
Order Desk: order capture, region-based pricing across six regions, GST invoicing, a sales dashboard and seven reports. Production: receiving, mixing, filling and dispatch, with live inventory and low-stock alerts. HRMS: attendance, leave and approvals.
Learning
Success metrics should be defined before launch. Order entry time was measured rigorously, whereas the reduction in stock-outs was tracked only informally.
