Building the system an ice cream company runs on

Three connected products, covering sales orders, production and HR, used every day by sales, kitchen, factory and office staff.

70–100orders a day through Order Desk
~85%less time per order (10–15 min → 1–2 min)
Fewerstock-outs and less wastage
Read the case study
Problem
Orders from cafés, hotels, retailers and distributors in six regions arrived as WhatsApp messages. Salespeople re-typed each one, which took 10–15 minutes, and production had no live view of demand. Stock planning was guesswork.
Users
15–20 salespeople and six regional heads; kitchen and factory staff; all 18 employees for HR.
My role
Noticed the problem, pitched the fix to the owner, wrote the specs, built the products with AI coding tools, rolled them out and trained the team.
Decisions
  • Kept WhatsApp as the starting point. Clients didn't have to change anything. Salespeople paste the message and an AI parser builds the order.
  • Never let a guess become a wrong order. Unclear flavour names go to the salesperson as a choice, and a keyword-based fallback keeps orders flowing if the AI service is down.
  • Connected sales and production. Weekly ingredient needs come from six weeks of real orders, and items are flagged below about 2.5 weeks of cover.
  • Recorded every stock movement instead of editable totals, so every number can be traced and audited.
What shipped
Order Desk: orders, pricing by city for six regions, GST invoices, a dashboard and seven reports. Ops: receiving, mixing, filling and dispatch, with live stock and alerts. HRMS: attendance, leave and approvals.
Next time
I'd agree on success measures before launch. Time saved per order was measured clearly; the drop in stock-outs was tracked only informally.
